Reference holdout checks hide the selected fingerprint and identical copies before scoring the remaining library. For measured evidence, choose Paste peaks or Upload file. Results clear when the input changes.

Input method

The selected entry and any identical fingerprints are withheld. Scores show how well the remaining library explains this known reference; this is a holdout check, not unknown-sample validation.

Use m/z:intensity pairs or one m/z,intensity pair per line. m/z 10–550, finite nonnegative intensities.

Up to 20 MB. Multi-record files are kept separate; wider JCAMP records show excluded peak counts.

METHODS & INTERPRETATION

Understand the result

Reproducible calculations with their limits stated alongside the evidence.

Validate & normalize

Finite, nonnegative intensities and at least one positive peak are required. m/z is binned to the nearest integer; half values round upward. Duplicate bins are summed. The largest peak becomes 100.

Manual/CSV peaks outside 10–550 are rejected. Wider JCAMP records are cropped with an explicit exclusion count. Compressed JCAMP formats are rejected.

Compare references

For measured inputs, cosine similarity is computed against every bundled reference. In a reference holdout check, the selected entry and identical fingerprints are removed before ranking. Cosine ranges from 0 to 1 and describes spectral shape, not an identity probability. Ties use library order; exact matches among the remaining references are listed.

Scores below 0.85 are labelled inconclusive. This is an unvalidated screening heuristic.

Fit candidate signals

Nonnegative least squares minimizes reconstruction error. Start with 60 cosine candidates; add up to 20 candidates from positive residuals in each of three passes. Exact duplicate spectra use a stable representative with alternative entry IDs.

All positive coefficients are reported. Similar spectra may produce nonunique solutions.

Review the residual

Residual = input − reconstruction. RMSE uses all 541 normalized intensity channels. R² retains negative values. Relative residual is residual norm divided by input norm.

Fit weights are normalized coefficients, not calibrated mass fractions, concentrations, or purity.

Independent evidence remains necessary. The platform does not infer toxicity, seizure provenance, novel compounds, or laboratory accreditation from spectral matching. Supporting attachments and review status are analyst-entered. The notebook activity log is not a tamper-proof chain of custody.

The imported category field was generated from compound-name rules; it is not presented as verified pharmacological classification. Synthetic self-matches and ratio sweeps do not establish unknown-sample performance.
